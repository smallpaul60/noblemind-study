## 1. Chapter_07.md — Remove false Mary Magdalene identification

The text incorrectly identifies the sinful woman of Luke 7:37 as Mary Magdalene. That is a Roman Catholic tradition with no textual support. Luke introduces Mary Magdalene separately in Luke 8:2 with no connection to this woman.

**Find:**
```
A woman identified by the text as a "sinner" -- traditionally understood to be Mary Magdalene -- crashed a dinner party at the home of a Pharisee named Simon.
```

**Replace with:**
```
A woman identified by the text only as "a sinner" -- unnamed, unidentified beyond her reputation -- crashed a dinner party at the home of a Pharisee named Simon.
```

## 2. Chapter_08.md — Fix Scripture misattributed to Jesus

The text attributes 1 Timothy 6:10 to Jesus ("He warned that..."), but those are Paul's words to Timothy. Jesus never said them. Replace with Jesus' own warning about money from Matthew 6:24.

**Find:**
```
He warned that "the love of money is a root of all sorts of evil" (1 Timothy 6:10). He told a rich young ruler to sell everything and give it to the poor -- and when the man walked away sad, Jesus did not chase him down and negotiate (Mark 10:21-22). He told His disciples bluntly: "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth" (Matthew 6:19). He declared that "it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God" (Mark 10:25).
```

**Replace with:**
```
He warned plainly: "No one can serve two masters; for either he will hate the one and love the other, or he will be devoted to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and wealth" (Matthew 6:24). He told a rich young ruler to sell everything and give it to the poor -- and when the man walked away sad, Jesus did not chase him down and negotiate (Mark 10:21-22). He told His disciples bluntly: "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth" (Matthew 6:19). He declared that "it is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God" (Mark 10:25).
```

## 3. Chapter_01.md — Replace denominational use of "pastor"

"Pastor" in the New Testament is the Greek word *poimen* (shepherd), another term for an elder/overseer — not the denominational usage where "pastor" means the preacher of a congregation. Replace with "preacher."

**Find:**
```
a president, a pastor, a parent
```

**Replace with:**
```
a president, a preacher, a parent
```
