## LULU INTERIOR PDF SPECIFICATIONS

### Trim Size
- **6" × 9"** (US Trade) — standard for non-fiction/Bible study

### PDF Page Size (with bleed)
- **6.25" × 9.25"** (adds 0.125" bleed on all sides)
- If no full-bleed images are used (likely the case for this text-heavy book), pages can be set at exactly 6" × 9" and Lulu will handle it. But 6.25" × 9.25" is safer.

### Margins
- **Outside/top/bottom:** 0.5" minimum from trim edge (safety margin)
- **Inside (gutter):** 0.7"–0.8" for a book of this length (~200–250 pages). The gutter compensates for binding.
- Use **mirror margins** (left/right pages have mirrored inside/outside margins)

### Fonts
- All fonts must be **embedded** in the PDF
- Suggested body font: Georgia, Garamond, or similar serif at 11–12pt
- Suggested heading font: Same family or complementary serif
- Line spacing: 1.3–1.5 for readability

### Page Numbering
- Front matter (half title through Table of Contents): lowercase Roman numerals or no numbers
- Body text (Prologue through end): Arabic numerals starting at 1
- Odd pages = right-hand side; even pages = left-hand side
- Title page, part title pages, and chapter opening pages: no visible page number (but counted)

### Headers/Footers
- Running headers: book title on left (verso) pages, chapter title on right (recto) pages
- Or: author name on left, chapter title on right
- No running header on chapter opening pages, part title pages, or front matter

### Other Requirements
- Single-page layout only (no spreads)
- No crop marks or registration marks
- Fonts embedded
- Images at 300 DPI minimum
- sRGB color space (Lulu converts to CMYK at print)
