Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you. Before you were born, I set you apart. These words are not poetry — they are a verdict, a record kept by the One who counts the hairs on every head and weighs every cause.

If God knew us before we drew breath, then no life is incidental. No pregnancy is a problem to be solved. Every child carries a name that was spoken in heaven before it was ever whispered on earth.
