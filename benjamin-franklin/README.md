# Benjamin Franklin (1812–1878) — Restoration Movement Preacher

Source materials by and about Elder Benjamin Franklin, second-generation Restoration Movement preacher, editor of *The American Christian Review*, and author of *The Gospel Preacher*. Not to be confused with the founding father of the same name.

## Copyright summary

- Everything Franklin wrote himself: **public domain.** US copyright on pre-1930 works expired. Quote, reformat, anthologize freely. Attribution is courteous but not legally required.
- Joseph Franklin & Headington's 1879 biography: **public domain.**
- Castleberry's 1963 *They Heard Him Gladly*: **in copyright** until 2058 (95 years from publication). Short quotations with attribution are fair use. Substantial excerpts would need permission from the rights holder (Old Paths Publishing Company / heirs).

## Total sermon count

41 sermons across the two volumes of *The Gospel Preacher* (20 in Vol. 1, 21 in Vol. 2), plus the shorter pieces and essays in *A Book of Gems* and *Biographical Sketch and Writings*.
