# The God Who Showed Up — Back Cover Blurb

---

When someone tells you their name, they are giving you access to who they are.

When God tells you His name, He is revealing His character — and inviting you to know Him.

God did not reveal all His names at once. He revealed each one at a specific moment — when His people needed to know that particular truth about who He is. To a slave woman alone in the desert, He revealed that He sees. To an old man with an impossible promise, He revealed that nothing is too hard. To a nation standing at bitter water, He revealed that He heals. To a man hiding in a winepress, He revealed that He is peace.

Every name answered a question His people were asking, whether they spoke it aloud or not.

*The God Who Showed Up* follows the names of God in the order He revealed them — not as a dictionary, but as a journey. The same journey Israel walked out of Egypt and into the promises of God. The same journey every Christian walks today.

From Elohim in Genesis 1 to Immanuel in Matthew 1, this book traces the progressive revelation of God's character through the crises, the promises, and the moments when everything seemed lost — and God showed up anyway.

He showed up for them. And He is here for us — now.
