New here? Start here.

A free workspace for examining Scripture on your own questions — on your own, or with any AI.

  1. Look up a passage. Type a reference in the Scripture box below (e.g., Acts 17:11) and click 📖 Bible Lookup.
  2. Test a claim with any AI. Click 🧪 Copy Claim Audit Prompt, then paste into Claude, Grok, Gemini, or ChatGPT along with the claim you want examined.
  3. Want the full tour? Open the User Guide to see everything this tool can do.
💡 Use Series to group related lessons together. The Scripture Index shows all references across your lessons.
Works with any AI chat. Based on user observations, Claude, Grok, and Gemini engage most consistently with these principle-loaded prompts. ChatGPT sometimes declines to quote specific passages mid-response — if that happens, try another AI.

💭 Search Scripture by Thought

Describe a thought in your own words — results are NASB passages related by meaning. Click one to open it.
💡 Finds related passages by meaning — never all of them. Scripture speaks on a subject through every passage that addresses it: open each result and read it in context. Semantic search provided by Bolls.Life (NASB).

📖 Search the Words of Scripture

Search the actual words of Scripture — every occurrence, in order, nothing ranked and nothing left out.
💡 This search is exhaustive: it returns every verse in which your word or phrase occurs, in Bible order. It finds words, not ideas — a passage that teaches your subject in different words will not appear, so search more than one word. Public-domain texts held offline; works with no connection.
