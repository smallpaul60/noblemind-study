# Integration Plan: Change the Mind, Change the Man & NobleMind Study Tool

## Principle
The book provides the map. The study tool provides the depth. Keep it simple — connect what already exists, don't build a second app.

## 1. Scripture Study Paths

Each chapter of the book centers on specific passages. Create a structured study path that:

- Lists the **primary passages** from each chapter
- Provides **cross-references** — other verses that address the same thought, letting Scripture interpret Scripture
- Links directly to the Noble Mind Study Tool for word-level Greek/Hebrew study (Strong's integration already exists)

### Example: Chapter 2 — The Progression

**Primary Passages:**
- James 1:14-15 — The anatomy of temptation (desire → sin → death)
- Proverbs 13:20 — The companion of fools will suffer harm
- Romans 6:16 — Slaves of the one you obey
- Romans 1:21-23 — The mind that turns from God
- Hebrews 3:13 — Hardened by the deceitfulness of sin

**Cross-References (Scripture Interpreting Scripture):**
- James 1:14-15 → Genesis 3:6 (Eve saw, desired, took — the same progression)
- James 1:14-15 → 2 Samuel 11:2-4 (David saw, sent, took — the same progression)
- Proverbs 13:20 → 1 Corinthians 15:33 (Bad company corrupts good morals)
- Proverbs 13:20 → Psalm 1:1 (Blessed is the man who does not walk/stand/sit)
- Romans 6:16 → John 8:34 (Everyone who commits sin is a slave of sin)
- Romans 1:21-23 → Ephesians 4:17-19 (Futility of mind, darkened understanding)
- Hebrews 3:13 → Hebrews 10:24-25 (Encourage one another day after day)

**Key Greek Terms for Study Tool:**
- *exelkomenos* — drawn out, lured (James 1:14)
- *deleazomenos* — baited, hooked (James 1:14)
- *syllabousa* — conceived (James 1:15)
- *doulos* — slave (Romans 6:16)
- *sklērunthē* — hardened (Hebrews 3:13)

This pattern repeats for all 10 chapters, creating a comprehensive study resource that goes deeper than the book alone.

## 2. Daily Reading Plan

A 30-day plan (3 days per chapter) structured as:

- **Day 1:** Read the chapter. Review the primary passages in context.
- **Day 2:** Study the cross-references. Let Scripture interpret Scripture.
- **Day 3:** Word study day. Look up the key Greek/Hebrew terms in the Study Tool. Sit with the text.

This gives the reader a month of sustained engagement with the material — the "daily discipline of being in God's Word" that Paul describes in his own story.

## 3. Direct Links from HTML Chapters

Each online chapter already exists as HTML. Add links at the bottom of each chapter (before the footer) that connect to:

- The relevant passages in the Noble Mind Study Tool
- The corresponding day(s) in the reading plan

This keeps the book and the tool connected without building anything new.

## 4. Implementation Priority

1. **Now:** Add Dedication, Author's Disclaimer, and Legal Disclaimer to the book's index.html (above chapter buttons)
2. **Next:** Build the cross-reference study paths for all 10 chapters
3. **Then:** Create the 30-day reading plan as a standalone page
4. **Later:** Add "Go Deeper" links at the bottom of each HTML chapter

## What This Is Not

- Not a clinical tool. No sobriety trackers, no risk assessments, no push notifications.
- Not a replacement for the book. The book is the voice. The tool is the library.
- Not a separate app. Everything lives in the existing NobleMind ecosystem, client-side, no server required.
