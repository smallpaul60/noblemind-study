## BACK COVER BLURB

The book tells you where to look. The Bible tells you what to see.

This ten-week Scripture study guide walks groups through *Change the Mind, Change the Man* one chapter at a time — but the book is not the curriculum. The Bible is. Every question sends the group into the biblical text. Every discussion begins and ends in Scripture.

**Two tracks. One room.** Each week includes questions for the person in the struggle and questions for the family carrying the weight — because these two audiences are living inside the same story, and they belong in the same conversation.

Week by week, the study follows the same arc as the book: the mind turning away from God, and the mind turning back. From the phone call to the Father who runs. From Romans 1 to Romans 6. From the pig pen to the road home.

Designed for prison ministries, reentry programs, congregational Bible studies, and families — wherever people are willing to open the text and stay in it.

**What this guide is:**
- Ten weeks of structured, Scripture-driven group discussion
- Dual-track questions for both the struggling and the family
- A consistent weekly rhythm any group leader can follow
- Hard questions with no pat answers — because the text doesn't offer any

**What this guide is not:**
- A self-help workbook
- A substitute for Scripture
- A program that promises easy resolution

The valley has a *through*. The road is long. And the text is already doing the work.

## ONLINE DESCRIPTION (Amazon, IngramSpark, etc.)

**Change the Mind, Change the Man: A Ten-Week Scripture Study Guide**
*For use with prison ministries, reentry programs, congregational studies, and families*

A companion study guide to *Change the Mind, Change the Man* by Paul Hainline. Ten weeks. Ten chapters. One Bible open on the table.

Each week, the group reads a chapter from the book — then goes straight to the Scriptures that chapter opens up, and stays there. The chapter is the door. Scripture is the room. Every question drives back to the biblical text.

What makes this guide different: **two tracks in every session.** Track A speaks to the one in the struggle. Track B speaks to the family. Both tracks study the same passages, in the same room, because addiction doesn't happen to one person — it happens to a family. And Scripture speaks to both.

Includes discussion questions, memory verses, weekly reading assignments, and practical guidance for group leaders. Designed for any setting where people are willing to sit with the text and let it do the work.

*Scripture quotations from the New American Standard Bible® (NASB).*
