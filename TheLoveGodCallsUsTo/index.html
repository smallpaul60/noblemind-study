The Love God Calls Us To

Walking Out 1 Corinthians 13

Paul Hainline

“But now faith, hope, love, abide these three; but the greatest of these is love.” — 1 Corinthians 13:13 (NASB)

A walk through 1 Corinthians 13 — the love chapter — taken not as a wedding text but as the apostle Paul's diagnostic for a fractured first-century church. Fifteen attributes of love, addressed one chapter at a time, with the Greek named where it helps, the Corinthian failures named where they sharpen the modern reader's seeing, and the love itself set forth as the eternal nature of God Himself, into which every believer is being called.
