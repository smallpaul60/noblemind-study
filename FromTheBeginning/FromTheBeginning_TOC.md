# From the Beginning: The Gospel from the Ground Up

## Table of Contents

---

### Part One — The Foundation
*Who is God, and why do you matter?*

**Chapter 1: Not an Accident**
God exists, He created with purpose, the universe isn't random. You were made in His image, known before you were born, and part of a plan that was in place before the world began.

**Chapter 2: Made in His Image**
What it means to bear the image of God. The spirit that makes you eternal, the mind that reflects His, the conscience that speaks, the freedom to choose, and the capacity for relationship.

**Chapter 3: What Went Wrong**
Sin entered — not just breaking rules, but a broken relationship. The choice, the consequence, the separation. Death as separation, not annihilation. The gap you cannot cross.

**Chapter 4: The Long Promise**
God didn't walk away. The thread of promise from Genesis 3:15 through Abraham, Judah, David, and the prophets. A portrait so specific that when the time came, there would be no mistaking who He was.

---

### Part Two — The Turning Point
*Who is Jesus, and what did He do?*

**Chapter 5: The Man Who Changed Everything**
Jesus arrives — the Word made flesh. What He claimed, what He demonstrated, and the life that backed it all up. Authority over nature, disease, death, and sin. The question everyone must answer.

**Chapter 6: The Death That Paid the Debt**
What the cross accomplished. The blood of the covenant, the certificate of debt nailed and canceled, the veil torn from top to bottom. The gap bridged by the only One who could pay the price.

**Chapter 7: The Empty Tomb**
The resurrection as historical fact. Why it matters — and what it proves about everything Jesus claimed.

---

### Part Three — The Response
*What does God ask you to do?*

**Chapter 8: So What Do I Do Now?**
The question the crowd asked at Pentecost. The consistent New Testament pattern: hear, believe, repent, confess, be baptized. Not a checklist — a faith response to what God has done.

**Chapter 9: What Happens Next?**
Added to the church — what the church actually is. The importance of gathering, growing, and serving.

**Chapter 10: The Life That Follows**
Living it out. Grace for stumbling. The ongoing relationship. The hope that carries you forward.
