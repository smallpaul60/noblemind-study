# From the Beginning: The Gospel from the Ground Up

## Project Overview
**Authors:** Paul & Pam
**Purpose:** A clear, honest, no-denominational-baggage presentation of the gospel starting from zero. Written for someone who has never opened a Bible and needs to understand the whole story — from creation to response.
**Target Audience:** Two generations — (1) parents who drifted from faith and stopped attending worship or reading Scripture, believing that simply believing in God is enough, and (2) their children who grew up with little to no knowledge of God or Scripture. Both groups are essentially starting from zero.
**Tone:** Conversational — like sitting across from someone at a coffee shop. Direct, warm, clear. Not academic, not preachy.
**Translation:** NASB (New American Standard Bible) as primary. Other translations referenced when helpful for comparison or clarity.
**Guiding Principles:**
- Scripture interprets Scripture
- Nothing but Scripture to back up assertions — no outside sources, no commentary, no denominational language
- Let the text speak for itself rather than imposing external systems
- Honest handling of difficult passages — willing to say "I don't know" when the text doesn't provide a clear answer
- No length constraints — the story determines the length
- Each chapter transitions naturally into the next

## Biblical Model for the Approach
- **Audience that knows Scripture:** Peter at Pentecost (Acts 2) — started with Joel and David, connected to Jesus
- **Audience that knows nothing:** Paul in Athens (Acts 17) — started with creation, built toward resurrection
- **Audience with partial knowledge:** Philip and the Ethiopian eunuch (Acts 8) — started where the reader already was (Isaiah), preached Christ from there
- This booklet follows the Paul-in-Athens model — starting from creation and building the complete story

## Key Theological Guardrails
- **Predestination:** God predetermined the PLAN (salvation through Christ), not the PEOPLE. The method was settled before creation; the invitation is open to all. Each person's response determines their participation. (2 Timothy 1:9, Ephesians 1:4 — "chose us IN HIM")
- **Faith and Works:** Grace saves. Faith responds. Obedience follows. Works don't earn salvation — they are the response to it. Faith without works is dead (James 2:24–26). Ephesians 2:8–10 must be read as a unit, not stopping at verse 9.
- **Tithing vs. Giving:** Tithing (specific percentage) was Law of Moses. New covenant giving is "as he may prosper" (1 Corinthians 16:2) and "cheerful" (2 Corinthians 9:7). Different covenants, different instructions.
- **Inherited Religion:** Faith passed down without personal examination from Scripture is tradition, not faith. Every generation must open the Book for themselves (Acts 17:11 — the Berean model).
- **The Church:** Not a denomination. The body of believers added by God (Acts 2:47). Identified by following the New Testament pattern, not by a name on a sign.
