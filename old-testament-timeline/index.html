Primary Source: The Holy Bible — Genesis through Malachi

Structural framework drawn from Bob Waldron, The Unfolding of God’s Plan and Outline for Classes: The Three Cycle Approach (Guardian of Truth Foundation, 2005). The thirteen-phase OT structure follows Waldron’s teaching; events and Scripture references are presented as Scripture records them.

Chronology: Hebrew Masoretic dating tradition. Exodus 1446 BC (1 Kings 6:1 anchor). Solomon’s temple foundation 967 BC. Fall of Samaria 722 BC. Final fall of Jerusalem 586 BC. First return 538/536 BC. Pre-patriarchal dates use the broader Ussher-style framework and should be read with a “circa.”
