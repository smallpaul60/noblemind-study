# Panel 5 — Love does not act unbecomingly

**Concept:** A school hallway at the lockers. Three teens are clustered in a tight huddle, snickering and whispering about a classmate walking past off-frame. The subject — a biracial teen girl — is the fourth in their friend group, but she has already taken a step away. Her body is turned toward the camera, her face composed and closed, her eyes not on the gossip target. She is walking on.

**Why this composition:** "Unbecoming behavior" is a category, not a single act, but for JH/HS audiences gossip is the canonical example — the small daily test of whether you'll join the snide huddle or step out of it. The composition shows the temptation (peer group leaning in, conspiratorial body language, eyes locked off-frame at a target) and the refusal (subject in the foreground already mid-step, body turned out, eyes forward). Per [[smallest-physical-fix-first]] — the *direction of her body* does the verse work; no expression-acrobatics needed.

**What makes it read as gossip and not just chatting:**

- Heads close, one girl with hand cupped to another's ear
- All three of the group looking off-frame in the same direction (at the target)
- Suppressed laughter — not open, not friendly
- Subject's eyes pointedly NOT looking the same direction as the group

## Note before you generate

This is the first panel where a teen is depicted doing something *wrong* (the gossip huddle), not just contrasting with absence of wrong. Both Gemini and ChatGPT can get squeamish about showing "mean" faces on kids and may soften the huddle into "three friends sharing a happy secret" — which kills the read. If that happens, the regen note is: **"keep the huddle's expressions snide and exclusionary, not friendly or playful."** Don't let the model neutralize them.
