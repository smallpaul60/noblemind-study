# Panel 3 — Love is not jealous

**Final concept (FINAL — locked):** Two teenage girls in the school art room. The Black girl's portrait drawing has just won first place; she is holding the blue ribbon in her own hands at lap level, looking down at it with shy pleasure. The white girl — also a serious art student with her own work on the easel beside her — is turned toward her, one hand lifted in a genuine "yes, you did it" gesture.

**Why this composition:** First pass (v1) had the white girl admiring the drawing — beautiful image, but read independently of the verse overlay it was just "two art-class friends." It needed an actual *moment of choice* in the frame. The ribbon held in the Black girl's own hands establishes unambiguously that she just won something coveted, and the white girl's open admiring gesture in response — knowing she didn't win — is the verse. Both girls being working artists in the same class makes the temptation to envy obvious; the white girl's body language shows she has refused it.

**v1 → final changes:**
- Added blue first-place ribbon — held in the Black girl's hands at lap level, looking down at it (NOT pinned to the drawing as v2 had it; "held in hands" is the simpler and stronger fix)
- Kept white girl's original "lifted hand, that's so good" gesture — the ribbon does the framing work, no need to engineer a clap
- Added explicit "fully clothed figure" directive on the drawing subject (Gemini produced nudity on the first generation of v1)

**Lesson for next panels:** When a panel doesn't read on its own, try the smallest physical addition first — an object in someone's hands often does more work than rebuilding the composition. My v2 over-engineered the fix (ribbon-on-drawing + clap-gesture); Paul's v3 ("just have her holding it") was cleaner and stronger.
