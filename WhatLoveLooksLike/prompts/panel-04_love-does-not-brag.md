# Panel 4 — Love does not brag, is not arrogant

**Concept:** Latino teen boy at a school awards-night podium, just received an academic-leadership award. Instead of facing the audience and basking, he is turned toward his project partner — a Korean-American teen girl off to the side of the stage — gesturing at her with an open palm, mid-sentence, crediting her. The trophy sits on the lectern in front of him, untouched.

**Why this composition:** "Does not brag" is the absence of self-promotion — hard to picture directly. The fix is to set up the moment where bragging would be the natural move (he just won, the audience is watching, the mic is in front of him) and show him doing the opposite. Every body-language cue inverts the boast posture: trophy *resting* on the lectern instead of held up, eyes on the *other person* instead of the audience, hand *open and outward* instead of closed and triumphant. The girl's surprised "who, me?" gesture confirms the read — she didn't expect to be named. (Per [[smallest-physical-fix-first]] — the trophy's *position* on the lectern carries the verse more than any expression note could.)

**Setting:** End-of-year school awards ceremony in a multipurpose room with a small portable stage. Warm directional light through high windows. Folding chairs with softly-applauding audience members in the background.
