# One Day Closer to Home — Back Jacket

---

**The world says the best is behind you.**

You've had your run. Enjoy the memories. Manage the decline. Be grateful for what was.

But what if the world is wrong?

*One Day Closer to Home* takes you into Scripture — to an old man holding a promise in his weathered hands, to an 84-year-old widow who never left her post, to an 85-year-old warrior who asked for the mountain with the giants on it. Then it walks you through the truths Paul and the apostles laid down for the final stretch — the body that is a seed waiting for glory, the tent that will give way to a building, the fear of death that has already been broken, and a weight of glory so heavy it buries the scale.

This is not a book about hanging in there. It is a book about turning around.

Because the sunrise ahead is better than anything behind you.

And every day — every single one — is one day closer to home.
